📝 English Important Questions

Important questions for examination

📚 Effective Learning Skills

Q1. What are Effective Learning Skills?

Answer: Effective learning skills are techniques and habits that help a student understand, remember and use information effectively.

Q2. Write any five effective learning skills.

Answer:

  1. Time Management
  2. Concentration
  3. Note Making
  4. Regular Revision
  5. Practice

Q3. Why is time management important for students?

Answer: Time management helps students complete their studies on time, reduce stress and maintain a proper balance between study and other activities.

Q4. Write some tips for effective learning.

Answer:

  • Set clear goals.
  • Prepare a study timetable.
  • Take short notes.
  • Revise regularly.
  • Practice questions.

🎧 Listening Comprehension

Q5. What is Listening Comprehension?

Answer: Listening comprehension is the ability to listen carefully, understand spoken information and respond correctly.

Q6. What are the steps of listening comprehension?

Answer:

  1. Listen carefully.
  2. Understand the main idea.
  3. Identify important information.
  4. Remember key details.
  5. Answer the questions.

Q7. Why is listening important?

Answer: Listening improves communication, concentration, vocabulary and understanding of spoken English.

📰 Newspaper Reading

Q8. What is Newspaper Reading?

Answer: Newspaper reading is the habit of reading news, articles, headlines and other information published in newspapers.

Q9. What are the benefits of newspaper reading?

Answer:

  • Improves vocabulary.
  • Improves reading skills.
  • Increases general knowledge.
  • Improves grammar.
  • Provides information about current events.

Q10. How does newspaper reading improve English?

Answer: Newspaper reading exposes us to new words, sentence structures and different styles of writing. Therefore, it improves vocabulary, grammar and reading skills.

🔤 Parts of Speech

Q11. What are Parts of Speech?

Answer: Parts of speech are categories of words according to their function and use in a sentence.

Q12. Name the eight Parts of Speech.

Answer:

  1. Noun
  2. Pronoun
  3. Verb
  4. Adjective
  5. Adverb
  6. Preposition
  7. Conjunction
  8. Interjection

Q13. What is a Noun? Give an example.

Answer: A noun is the name of a person, place, thing or idea.

Example: Nitin, Delhi, Book, Honesty.

Q14. What is a Pronoun? Give an example.

Answer: A pronoun is a word used in place of a noun.

Example: He, She, It, They.

Q15. What is an Adjective?

Answer: An adjective is a word that describes a noun or pronoun.

Example: A beautiful flower.

Q16. What is an Adverb?

Answer: An adverb describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.

Example: He runs quickly.

Q17. What is a Preposition?

Answer: A preposition shows the relationship between words.

Example: in, on, at, under, behind.

Q18. What is a Conjunction?

Answer: A conjunction joins words, phrases or sentences.

Example: and, but, or, because.

Q19. What is an Interjection?

Answer: An interjection expresses sudden feelings or emotions.

Example: Wow!, Oh!, Hurrah!, Alas!

✍️ Sentence Structure

Q20. What is Sentence Structure?

Answer: Sentence structure means the arrangement of words in a sentence to express a complete meaning.

Q21. What is the basic structure of a sentence?

Answer:

Subject + Verb + Object

Example:
Nitin + plays + cricket.

Q22. What are the types of sentences?

Answer:

  1. Assertive Sentence
  2. Interrogative Sentence
  3. Imperative Sentence
  4. Exclamatory Sentence

Q23. Give examples of different types of sentences.

Assertive: I am a student.

Interrogative: Are you a student?

Imperative: Please open the door.

Exclamatory: What a beautiful day!

🔵 Verb

Q24. What is a Verb?

Answer: A verb is a word that shows an action or state of being.

Examples: Run, Eat, Write, Play, Read.

Q25. What is a Main Verb?

Answer: A main verb shows the main action of a sentence.

Example: She plays cricket.

Q26. What is a Helping Verb?

Answer: A helping verb helps the main verb in a sentence.

Examples: is, am, are, was, were, has, have, will.

Q27. What is a Transitive Verb?

Answer: A transitive verb requires an object to complete its meaning.

Example: He reads a book.

Q28. What is an Intransitive Verb?

Answer: An intransitive verb does not require an object.

Example: The baby sleeps.
