English Vocabulary

Important words and their meanings

What is Vocabulary?

Vocabulary is the collection of words that a person knows and uses in a language.

Important Words

  1. Accurate - Correct and exact.
  2. Achieve - To successfully reach a goal.
  3. Brilliant - Very intelligent or excellent.
  4. Confident - Having belief in yourself.
  5. Efficient - Working well without wasting time.
  6. Essential - Very important or necessary.
  7. Improve - To make something better.
  8. Knowledge - Information and understanding.
  9. Opportunity - A chance to do something.
  10. Successful - Achieving the desired result.

Synonyms

  • Happy → Joyful
  • Big → Large
  • Fast → Quick
  • Begin → Start
  • Important → Significant

Antonyms

  • Hot → Cold
  • Big → Small
  • Easy → Difficult
  • Early → Late
  • Strong → Weak

Tips to Improve Vocabulary

  • Read books and newspapers.
  • Learn a few new words every day.
  • Use new words in sentences.
  • Learn synonyms and antonyms.
  • Practice regularly.
