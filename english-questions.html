English Important Questions

Important questions for examination

Important Questions

  1. What is Grammar? Explain its importance.

  2. Explain the eight parts of speech with examples.

  3. Explain the three types of Tenses.

  4. What are the different types of sentences?

  5. Differentiate between Active Voice and Passive Voice.

  6. What is Essay Writing? Explain its basic structure.

  7. What are the important parts of a formal email?

  8. What is Vocabulary? How can you improve it?

  9. What are Synonyms and Antonyms? Give examples.

  10. Write a short paragraph on the importance of education.

Quick Revision

  • Grammar → Rules of a language
  • Noun → Name of a person, place or thing
  • Verb → Shows action or state
  • Adjective → Describes a noun
  • Synonym → Similar meaning
  • Antonym → Opposite meaning
  • Essay → Introduction + Body + Conclusion
