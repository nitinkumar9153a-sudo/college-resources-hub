📚 English PYQ Practice

Previous-question style practice for examination

📖 Effective Learning Skills

Q1. What are Effective Learning Skills?

Answer: Effective learning skills are techniques and habits that help students understand, remember and use information effectively.

Q2. Explain the importance of Effective Learning Skills.

Answer: Effective learning skills help students improve concentration, manage time, understand concepts, remember information and perform better in examinations.

Q3. Write five techniques for effective learning.

Answer:

  1. Time Management
  2. Note Making
  3. Regular Revision
  4. Practice
  5. Self-Assessment

🎧 Listening Comprehension

Q4. Define Listening Comprehension.

Answer: Listening comprehension is the ability to listen carefully, understand spoken information and respond correctly.

Q5. What are the steps involved in Listening Comprehension?

Answer:

  1. Listen carefully.
  2. Understand the main idea.
  3. Identify important details.
  4. Remember key information.
  5. Answer the questions.

Q6. Write the importance of listening skills.

Answer: Listening skills improve communication, concentration, vocabulary and understanding of spoken English.

📰 Newspaper Reading

Q7. What is Newspaper Reading?

Answer: Newspaper reading is the habit of reading news, articles, headlines and other information published in newspapers.

Q8. Explain the benefits of Newspaper Reading.

Answer:

  • Improves vocabulary.
  • Improves reading skills.
  • Increases general knowledge.
  • Improves grammar.
  • Provides information about current events.

Q9. How can a student improve English through newspapers?

Answer: A student can learn new words, understand sentence structures, read articles regularly and note down difficult vocabulary. This improves English language skills.

🔤 Parts of Speech

Q10. What are Parts of Speech?

Answer: Parts of speech are categories of words according to their function and use in a sentence.

Q11. Name the eight Parts of Speech.

Answer:

  1. Noun
  2. Pronoun
  3. Verb
  4. Adjective
  5. Adverb
  6. Preposition
  7. Conjunction
  8. Interjection

Q12. Identify the Parts of Speech in the sentence:

Nitin plays cricket.

Answer:

Nitin → Noun
plays → Verb
cricket → Noun

Q13. Identify the Parts of Speech:

She is a beautiful girl.

Answer:

She → Pronoun
is → Verb
a → Article
beautiful → Adjective
girl → Noun

✍️ Sentence Structure

Q14. What is Sentence Structure?

Answer: Sentence structure means the arrangement of words in a sentence to express a complete meaning.

Q15. Write the basic structure of a sentence with an example.

Subject + Verb + Object

Nitin + plays + cricket.

Q16. Name the types of sentences.

Answer:

  1. Assertive Sentence
  2. Interrogative Sentence
  3. Imperative Sentence
  4. Exclamatory Sentence

Q17. Identify the type of sentence:

1. I am a student.
2. Are you ready?
3. Please open the door.
4. What a beautiful day!

Answer:

1. Assertive
2. Interrogative
3. Imperative
4. Exclamatory

🔵 Verb

Q18. Define Verb with examples.

Answer: A verb is a word that shows an action or state of being.

Examples: Run, Eat, Write, Play, Read.

Q19. What is a Main Verb?

Answer: A main verb shows the main action in a sentence.

Example: She plays cricket.

Q20. What is a Helping Verb?

Answer: A helping verb helps the main verb in a sentence.

Examples: is, am, are, was, were, has, have, will.

Q21. Differentiate between Transitive and Intransitive Verbs.

Answer:

Transitive Verb: It requires an object.
Example: He reads a book.

Intransitive Verb: It does not require an object.
Example: The baby sleeps.

📌 Additional Practice

Q22. What are Tenses?

Answer: Tense shows the time of an action. The three main tenses are Present, Past and Future.

Q23. What are Articles?

Answer: Articles are words used before nouns.

The three articles are: A, An and The.

Q24. What are Prepositions?

Answer: Prepositions show the relationship between words.

Examples: in, on, at, under, behind.
