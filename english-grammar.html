English Grammar

Basic English Grammar Notes

What is Grammar?

Grammar is a set of rules that helps us use words and sentences correctly in a language.

Parts of Speech

  1. Noun - Name of a person, place, thing or idea.
  2. Pronoun - Used in place of a noun.
  3. Verb - Shows an action or state.
  4. Adjective - Describes a noun or pronoun.
  5. Adverb - Describes a verb, adjective or adverb.
  6. Preposition - Shows relationship between words.
  7. Conjunction - Joins words or sentences.
  8. Interjection - Expresses sudden feelings.

Types of Tenses

  • Present Tense
  • Past Tense
  • Future Tense

Types of Sentences

  1. Declarative Sentence
  2. Interrogative Sentence
  3. Imperative Sentence
  4. Exclamatory Sentence

Active and Passive Voice

Active Voice: The subject performs the action.

Example: Ram writes a letter.

Passive Voice: The action is performed on the subject.

Example: A letter is written by Ram.
