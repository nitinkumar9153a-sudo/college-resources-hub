English Writing Skills

Improve your writing and communication skills

What is Writing?

Writing is a way of expressing ideas, thoughts and information through written words.

Types of Writing

  1. Essay Writing
  2. Letter Writing
  3. Email Writing
  4. Report Writing
  5. Paragraph Writing
  6. Notice Writing

Essay Writing

An essay is a piece of writing that presents ideas or information about a particular topic.

Basic Structure

  • Introduction
  • Main Body
  • Conclusion

Email Writing

A formal email should be clear, polite and properly structured.

  • Subject
  • Greeting
  • Main Message
  • Closing
  • Name

Tips for Better Writing

  • Use simple and clear language.
  • Use correct grammar.
  • Organize your ideas properly.
  • Avoid unnecessary repetition.
  • Check spelling before submitting.
