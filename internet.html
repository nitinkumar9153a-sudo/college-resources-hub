Internet and WWW

What is Internet?

The Internet is a global network of interconnected computers and devices that communicate with each other.

What is WWW?

WWW stands for World Wide Web. It is a service that allows users to access web pages and information through the Internet.

Internet vs WWW

  • Internet: A global network of computers.
  • WWW: A service that runs on the Internet.

Applications of Internet

  • Online education
  • Online shopping
  • Online banking
  • Entertainment
  • Communication
