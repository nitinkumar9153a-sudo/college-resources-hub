📚 Physics Previous Year Questions

Previous year and practice questions for Physics

📝 Important PYQ Topics

Wave Optics

1. Explain Huygens principle with a neat diagram.

2. Explain Newton's rings and derive the required expression.

3. Explain Fraunhofer diffraction from a single slit.

4. Explain diffraction grating and resolving power.

Lasers

1. Explain spontaneous and stimulated emission.

2. Explain population inversion and pumping.

3. Explain Ruby laser with diagram.

4. Explain He-Ne laser with diagram.

Electromagnetic Waves

1. Derive the continuity equation.

2. Write Maxwell's equations in vacuum.

3. Explain Poynting vector.

4. Explain Poynting theorem.

Quantum Mechanics

1. Explain de-Broglie hypothesis.

2. Derive time-dependent Schrödinger equation.

3. Explain Born interpretation of wave function.

4. Explain Heisenberg uncertainty principle.

Applications of Schrödinger Equation

1. Explain particle in one-dimensional box.

2. Derive energy eigenvalues for a particle in 1-D box.

3. Explain quantum tunneling.

4. Explain quantum harmonic oscillator qualitatively.

Crystal Structures

1. Explain SC, BCC and FCC structures.

2. Calculate coordination number and packing factor.

3. Explain Frenkel and Schottky defects.

4. Differentiate between Frenkel and Schottky defects.

Free Electron Theory

1. Explain classical free electron theory.

2. Explain the failures of classical free electron theory.

3. Explain Fermi-Dirac distribution.

4. Explain Fermi energy and density of states.
